FAQ

Questions, answered

Where do you deliver?

We deliver across {{ service_area }}. Delivery is $4.95, and free on orders over $35. If you're outside our area, choose pickup or contact us — we're expanding.

How does pickup work?

Choose pickup at checkout and pick a preferred day. We'll confirm your pickup window by email or text, then share the exact address. Pickup is always free.

When are the greens harvested?

The morning of your pickup or delivery day — never before. That's why we ask for a preferred day at least 7 days out: it lets us time the harvest perfectly.

How long do microgreens stay fresh?

Because they're harvested to order, our greens typically stay crisp 7–10 days refrigerated. Keep them in their clamshell, unwashed, and rinse just before eating.

Are your greens really organic?

Yes. We grow from organic seed in organic growing medium with filtered water — no pesticides, herbicides, or synthetic fertilizers, ever.

Do you offer subscriptions or wholesale?

The Weekly Harvest Box is our most popular repeat order. For restaurants, juice bars, or meal-prep businesses needing regular supply, get in touch — we offer wholesale pricing.

How do I pay?

Secure online card payment at checkout. If online payment isn't enabled yet, you can pay by card, cash, or tap on pickup/delivery — your order is still reserved.
